INTERPRETATION GUIDE
================================================================================
Participation Coefficient (PC):
  - Measures diversity of intermodular connections
  - Range: 0 (all within-module) to 1 (evenly distributed)
  - Connector nodes: PC > 0.62

Within-Module Degree Z-Score:
  - Standardized within-module degree
  - Hub nodes: Z > 2.5

Partition Distance Metrics:
  - VI: Variation of Information (0=identical, higher=more different)
  - NMI: Normalized Mutual Information (1=identical, 0=different)
  - ARI: Adjusted Rand Index (1=identical, 0=random)

Node Roles (Guimera & Amaral 2005):
  1. Ultra-peripheral: Z<2.5, P<0.05
  2. Peripheral: Z<2.5, 0.05<P<0.62
  3. Satellite connector: Z<2.5, 0.62<P<0.80
  4. Kinless: Z<2.5, P>0.80
  5. Provincial hub: Z>2.5, P<0.30
  6. Connector hub: Z>2.5, 0.30<P<0.75
  7. Global hub: Z>2.5, P>0.75
